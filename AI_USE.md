# AI USE — sessions GEMSDOE89

Required by the competition rules (generative-AI disclosure in
https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf as cited in the brief).
This section is factual, not promotional: it separates what the agent produced
from what the human decided.

## What the AI wrote (SPACING session, 2026-10-23)

- `gemsdoe/spacing.py` — the SPACING-axis mechanics: seeded greedy
  violator/anchor split, margin-extended EDT candidate scoring, Poisson-disk,
  along-strike and partial square-lattice placement with the parent's 2.828 px
  separation floor, and the spacing/alignment statistics.
- `tools/build_spacing.py` — pinned-input verification, the three variants,
  twin writing, re-read gates, REPORT-ONLY instruments and build reports.
- `tools/fetch_inputs.py` — reproducible, sha256-verified fetch of the pinned
  owner-mirror inputs via the GitHub API (no DrivenData access).
- `tests/test_spacing.py` — six synthetic-frame tests including non-vacuous
  "the arm must act" guards and the global separation-floor assertion.
- `ledger/MECHANISM_CARDS_SPACING.md`, and the 2026-10-23 updates to
  `SLOT_PLAN.md`, `README.md` (CURRENT STATE only), `docs/SUBMIT.md`,
  `IRREGULARITIES.md`, this file, and the `registry/` report-only artifacts.
- `tools/build_ledger.py` was extended to consume SPACING reports; RELIEF rows
  are regenerated byte-identically (verified by diff).

## What the AI decided (SPACING session; the human can reverse)

- **Axis = SPACING**, because every other named weekly-slot axis already had
  ledger `(axis, value)` pairs and TRACKB is Phase 2-only.
- The three constructions and their strengths (r_min 3.0 px, windows 6/5/4 px,
  lattice s = 3 / tolerance 1.5, strike |cos| gate 0.5), chosen from R0's
  measured NN geometry (min 2.828 px; 30.3 % of dots at that distance).
- The MODEL predictions and bands, derived from the marginal-gain algebra with
  an explicit surrogate-scale assumption (c = 1.78e-5); they are not scores.
- A first build that created 1 px neighbour pairs was identified, discarded and
  rebuilt under the 2.828 px acceptance floor (IRREGULARITIES item 9).

## What the AI decided (and the human can reverse)

- **Axis = RELIEF.** The brief told the agent not to ask which axis to use. The
  choice was made from the recovered ledger: MASS (3), PRUNE (7), SNAP (3) and
  CONCENTRATION (3) already had `(axis, value)` pairs on this parent; RELIEF had
  none.
- **Source/target classes** were chosen from the pinned catalogue's density, not
  from any test label.
- **Destination rule** (structure-gated gap filling) was chosen after a first
  build placed the added dots on flat ground (mean |∇det_elev| 0.66 vs the
  parent's 13.6), which would have tested placement quality rather than relief.
  That first build was discarded and is not in the ledger.
- The **predicted changes and their bands** are the agent's MODEL, built from
  the marginal-gain algebra with an explicit, stated assumption range. They are
  not scores.

  transmitted.

  prior, and for a REPORT-ONLY proxy.
