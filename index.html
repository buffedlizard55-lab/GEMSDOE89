Why the parent ranked well locally

Verified reconstruction; score causality remains unknown

The cited 0.2778 for h33-h33-2-b2 is a CLAIM, not an organizer receipt in this repository. Its raster is reproducible: the recorded recipe thins the pinned h19_5 corroborated ridge field, then prunes using the known-fault catalogue, leaving 37,654 unit dots. Multi-layer lineament continuity, reduced redundancy, and catalogue pruning are plausible contributors, but the score alone cannot identify the cause. User-provided leaderboard snapshots of 0.3195 and 0.4413 conflict in time/provenance and have not been independently confirmed here.

Official scoring uses the organizer's distance-weighted Tversky metric; see the competition description. Official USGS work supports testing multiple geophysical indicators in blind geothermal play-fairway settings, but does not make any one layer a fault label.
